From Above

Drone Coverage

Our licensed drone pilots capture breathtaking aerial perspectives of your venue, ceremony, guest arrivals, processions, and outdoor celebrations, adding scale and cinematic context to your wedding film.

From sweeping establishing shots to intimate aerial portraits of the couple, drone footage helps connect the location, decor, and emotion into one immersive visual story.

We plan every aerial shot around safety, venue permissions, weather, and the flow of your events. The result is polished drone footage that feels purposeful, elegant, and fully connected to the rest of your wedding film.

Drone coverage is especially powerful for destination venues, outdoor mandaps, beach ceremonies, and grand processions. It gives your film a sense of scale while keeping the focus on your story and celebration.
