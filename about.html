Our Beginning

The EverAfter Story

EverAfter Studios was born from a single belief: every love story deserves to be told beautifully and honestly. Founded in 2012 by lead photographer Rajan Menon, what began as a small one-man studio has grown into an award-winning collective of photographers, filmmakers, editors, and production coordinators.

Over 850 weddings and 12 years later, we remain driven by the same purpose: to create images and films that make you feel every emotion again decades from now. Our work is shaped by preparation, quiet observation, beautiful light, and respect for each family's traditions.

We believe weddings are sacred, layered, and fast-moving. Our job is to be present without taking over, to guide when guidance helps, and to quietly preserve the moments you planned as well as the ones you never saw coming.

How We Work

Our Creative Approach

A simple process keeps the experience clear from first call to final delivery.

01
Discovery Call

We begin with a detailed conversation about your story, vision, venue, rituals, family priorities, and the moments that matter most to you.

02
Pre-Wedding Shoot

A comfortable session helps you get
used to the camera, understand simple
posing cues, and build trust with the team before the main event.

03
Wedding Day

We arrive early, coordinate quietly with your planner, protect the timeline, and document everything from morning preparations to the final celebration.

04
Delivery & Heirloom

You receive a teaser gallery within 48 hours, a full edited gallery in 3 to 4 weeks, and guidance for albums, prints, and family keepsakes.
